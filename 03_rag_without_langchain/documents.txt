Supervised learning trains a model on labeled data to predict outputs for new inputs.
Unsupervised learning finds hidden patterns in data that has no labels.
K-means clustering groups data points into K clusters around centre points.
Overfitting happens when a model memorizes training data and performs poorly on new data.
Cross-validation splits data into folds to estimate how well a model will generalize.
Random forests combine many decision trees to make more accurate predictions.
Gradient descent updates model weights step by step to reduce the loss.
Neural networks are made of layers of connected neurons that learn complex patterns.
Embeddings turn text into vectors so that similar meanings are close together.
Retrieval-augmented generation finds relevant documents and gives them to a language model as context.
